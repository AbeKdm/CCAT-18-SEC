# CCAT Timer - User Guide

## Overview

CCAT Timer is a specialized countdown timer designed for the CCAT (Criteria Cognitive Aptitude Test). It provides:
- **18-second rounds** (average time per question)
- **Automatic looping** through multiple rounds
- **Audio cues** to help manage time
- **Touch-optimized interface** for quick access during testing

## Controls

### START Button (Green)
- **Function**: Begins the 18-second countdown
- **When to use**: At the start of your test or after a pause
- **Disabled**: When timer is already running
- **Visual feedback**: Becomes opaque when pressed

### STOP Button (Red)
- **Function**: Pauses the countdown
- **When to use**: If you need to pause (not recommended during real test)
- **Disabled**: When timer is not running
- **Visual feedback**: Becomes opaque when pressed

### RE Button (Center, Large)
- **Function**: Restarts the current 18-second round
- **When to use**:
  - When you finish a question early and want to move to the next
  - If you need to restart due to confusion
  - During practice to test yourself
- **Always enabled**: Can be used anytime
- **Visual feedback**: Pulses during countdown, responsive to touch

## Sound Cues

The timer uses 4 distinct audio signals to keep you on track:

### 1. Halfway Beep (9 seconds remaining)
- **Sound**: Single medium-pitched beep
- **Purpose**: Mark the halfway point of your time
- **Action**: You should be finalizing your answer

### 2. Final Countdown (5-1 seconds remaining)
- **Sound**: One beep per second
- **Purpose**: Alert you that time is running out
- **Action**: Quickly finalize and mark your answer

### 3. Round End (0 seconds)
- **Sound**: Three rapid beeps in quick succession
- **Purpose**: Indicates the 18 seconds have elapsed
- **Action**: Move to the next question immediately

### 4. Silence (Between 10-6 seconds)
- **Sound**: No audio
- **Purpose**: Avoid distraction while you work

## Workflow During Test

### Before the Test
1. Have device at comfortable height/angle
2. Ensure volume is at comfortable level
3. Run a quick test round to familiarize yourself

### During Each Question
1. Read the question
2. Press **START** if timer isn't already running
3. Work on the question while watching the countdown
4. When done:
   - Press **RE** to move to next question, OR
   - Wait for the 3 beeps if you need full time

### Between Questions
- The timer automatically resets to 18 seconds
- Audio cue signals when next round begins
- No manual reset needed unless you press **RE**

### Monitoring Time
- **Large number**: Current round time remaining
- **Top right**: Total time spent so far
- **Status indicator**: Shows if timer is running/paused

## Tips for Success

### Time Management
- **Don't watch the timer constantly** - focus on the question
- **Listen to the audio cues** - they tell you when to finalize
- **At 9 seconds**: You should have an answer selected
- **At 5 seconds**: Definitely make your final choice

### Audio Settings
- **Enable sound**: Ensure device volume is on
- **Mute notifications**: Disable other app notifications
- **Headphones optional**: Beeps work through speaker or headphones

### Practice
- **First 2 questions**: Get comfortable with the flow
- **Questions 3-50**: Use the timer as your guide
- **Don't panic**: If timer reaches 0, move to next question anyway

### Troubleshooting

**No sound?**
- Check device volume isn't muted
- Ensure app has audio permissions
- Try re-running a round (3 beeps at end)

**Timer not advancing?**
- Ensure START button was pressed
- Check if device is locked or minimized
- Restart the app

**Time seems wrong?**
- Verify system time is correct on device
- Reset timer with RE button

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this during the official CCAT?**
A: Check with your test administrator first. Some organizations allow external timers, others don't.

**Q: What if I finish a question in 5 seconds?**
A: Press the **RE** button immediately to move to the next question.

**Q: Can I pause between questions?**
A: Press STOP, but remember you lose time. Better to press RE when ready.

**Q: Does it work offline?**
A: Yes! No internet required.

**Q: Can I go back to previous questions?**
A: The timer doesn't navigate between questions - that's up to the test platform.

**Q: Is 18 seconds right for my test?**
A: Check your specific test format. Standard CCAT = 50Q in 15min ≈ 18 seconds/question.
